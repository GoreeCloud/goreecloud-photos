# GoreeCloud Photos — Competitive Objectives

**Status:** Product objectives, not comparative performance claims.

GoreeCloud Photos should compete on durable user control rather than lock-in or engagement manipulation.

## Objectives

### Self-hosted independence

Provide a complete ordinary photo-management path on GoreeCloud-controlled infrastructure without requiring an external processing service for backup, browsing, organization, search, or recovery.

### Transparent backup truth

Make device-only, in-flight, server-stored, offline, optimized, shared, protected, and failed states understandable so users do not mistake local presence for verified backup.

### Original-media preservation

Treat uploaded originals as immutable and keep generated derivatives rebuildable wherever practical.

### Portability

Make selected assets, albums, date ranges, and complete authorized libraries exportable with originals and portable metadata.

### Recovery

Design for tested restore, integrity verification, disaster recovery, migration, and long-term preservation rather than treating synchronization as backup.

### Privacy-controlled intelligence

Keep face recognition, people clustering, OCR, object recognition, location processing, semantic search, and AI assistance optional and independently revocable.

### Local-first usability

Allow useful access to downloaded originals, cached media, favorites, albums, edits, and pending changes during connectivity loss.

### Family ownership clarity

Support shared household spaces without silently transferring ownership or confusing personal and shared library boundaries.

### Graceful degradation

Preserve original media access when optional search, intelligence, preview, or secondary processing systems are unavailable.

No competitive objective should be represented as achieved until the relevant implementation and evidence exist.
